# NAME
eternalforest-by-soulman - Eternal Forest, a 4k procedural graphics executable

# SYNOPSIS
**eternalforest-by-soulman**

# DESCRIPTION
**Eternal Forest** is a 4-kilobyte executable graphics production (exegfx) by Soulman, released in July 2020.
It was ranked 5th in the 4k procedural graphics competition at Solskogen 2020.

A raymarched forest with a bird is rendered by a GLSL fragment shader in a fullscreen window. An OpenGL 4.5 capable driver is needed. Depending on the graphics card and the resolution, the picture can take a long time to appear, and the desktop may not respond during the rendering.

Exit using the **Esc** key or your window manager's close window shortcut.

# OPTIONS
There are no options to this program.
